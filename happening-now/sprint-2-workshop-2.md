Today we covered how agent systems are built and how to make them reliable. Now it's your turn.

**Slides:** [view the deck](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1jLAcyA7yE66R9Cavj-BCQW5799HABCPSxpjWe55CKDc/edit?usp=sharing)

#### Your project

- Pick a real person with a weekly task that's slow or error-prone, and build one complete workflow for it.
- Start with the simplest version that works (a single call or a workflow) before reaching for agents.
- Collect 10+ real examples with known good answers, and test against them.
- Demo it end to end in under 3 minutes, with sources for every claim and what it gets wrong.

Need an idea? The deck lists five starting points, like a meeting-to-task assistant or a submission reviewer.
